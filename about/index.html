Our Living Heritage

About Nallayil Ayurveda

A tradition of healing born in Kerala, dedicated to restoring harmony in mind, body, and spirit through classical Ayurvedic medical wisdom and genuine human empathy.

The Healing Journey

Ancient Foundations, Modern Clinical Standards

Located in the peaceful town of Mullampara, Manjeri, Nallayil Ayurveda Heritage Hospital & Ayur Home was founded with an unyielding commitment: to practice Ayurveda in its purest, most authentic classical form, while maintaining the highest diagnostic rigour of modern medicine.

Under the guidance of experienced Ayurvedic physicians and Marma Chikitsa masters, our hospital has become a beacon of non-surgical recovery for disc herniation, sciatica, osteoarthritis, stroke rehabilitation, and chronic lifestyle disorders.

We view healing not as a temporary suppression of symptoms, but as a deep recalibration of your biological Agni (digestive fire) and Tridoshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha).

Our Guiding Principles

What Defines Nallayil

Shuddhata (Purity)

We source only pure, pesticide-free, wild-crafted herbs and prepare classical Kashayams in traditional brass and clay vessels to preserve maximum prana and medicinal potency.

Karuna (Compassion)

Every patient is heard with patience and treated like family. Our physicians dedicate ample consultation time to listen to your entire health journey and emotional well-being.

Nischayam (Diagnostic Precision)

We integrate classical Ashtasthana Pariksha (eight-fold examination including pulse diagnosis) with contemporary MRI scans and blood tests for foolproof clinical diagnosis.

Sukhada (Ayur Home Sanctuary)

Our inpatient Ayur Home rooms provide a serene, tranquil haven where peaceful sleep, Ayurvedic organic nutrition, and meditative silence speed up cellular regeneration.

Our Hospital Campus & Infrastructure

Equipped with comprehensive facilities designed for comfortable long-stay inpatient recoveries and outpatient consultations.

Inpatient (IP) Ayur Home Rooms
