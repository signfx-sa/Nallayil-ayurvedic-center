Getting Here

Nallayil Ayurveda Heritage Hospital & Ayur Home is conveniently located at Mullampara, Manjeri, Malappuram district. The campus is easily reachable from Manjeri town centre, Calicut International Airport (CCJ - approx. 22 km), and nearby railway stations.
