Spine & Vital Points

Marma Chikitsa (മർമ്മ ചികിത്സ)

Marma Chikitsa is Kerala's ancient science of stimulating the 107 vital energy intersections (Marmas) where muscle, nerve, bone, and life force (prana) converge. Traumatic injuries, postural strain, and compressed discs often cause energy stagnation and severe radiating pain.

Key Clinical Indications:
Trapped Nerves Sciatica Radiation Whiplash Injury Sports Sprains Frozen Shoulder Postural Deformity
Recommended: 7 to 21 Days Book Marma Consultation

Cellular Bio-Purification

Classical Panchakarma Detox

Panchakarma is the complete 5-fold biological reset. Beginning with preparatory oleation (Snehana) and steam sweating (Swedana), internal toxins (Ama) are mobilized and gently eliminated through therapeutic emesis, purgation, herbal enemas (Vasti), nasal administration (Nasya), and bloodletting.

Key Clinical Indications:
Chronic Fatigue Autoimmune Disorders Metabolic Syndrome Liver Sluggishness Premature Aging
Recommended: 7, 14 or 21 Days Book Panchakarma Session
