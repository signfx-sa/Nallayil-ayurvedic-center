COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to help you prepare for your healing journey at Nallayil.

How do I choose the right treatment for me?

You do not need to diagnose yourself beforehand. If you are unsure, book a general consultation; our doctors will evaluate your constitution and recommend the ideal treatment path.

Do I need a consultation before starting a treatment?

Yes. Classical Ayurveda requires an initial physician assessment to ensure the oils and intensity selected match your body type and medical history.

How long does a typical treatment session take?

Individual daily therapies usually range from 45 to 90 minutes, while comprehensive Panchakarma programs span 7, 14, or 21 consecutive days.

Can therapies be personalized if I have multiple issues?

Absolutely. Our treatment programs are entirely customized, addressing layered concerns such as lower back pain accompanied by chronic stress or digestive sluggishness.

How can I book an appointment?

You can click the 'Book Now' button to fill out our simple booking sheet, or directly message our care team via WhatsApp for instant assistance.
